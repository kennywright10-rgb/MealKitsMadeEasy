The Method

If you are making dinner from scratch, why not make five?

That one question is the whole idea. You cook one dinner tonight and bag the other four for the freezer. Sauces made, veggies chopped, proteins trimmed. Do that once a week and within a few months you are only cooking from scratch about four nights a month.

Pick four recipes

Choose four dinners each month. Mix chicken, beef, pork and seafood so the freezer never gets boring.

Make five of each

One for dinner tonight, four for later. You are already chopping and measuring, so the extra kits go fast.

Bag it by number

Every recipe is written bag by bag. Bag 1, Bag 2, Bag 3. Fill them, tuck them together, and freeze.

Thaw and cook

Pull a kit the night before or thaw it in warm water in 15 to 20 minutes. The prep is already done.

Our Story

Twenty years of getting families to the table

My husband Ken and I opened Dinner A'Fare in Cumming, Georgia in 2004. Our goal never changed: get families together around the dinner table without the stress of figuring out what to make.

  • 2004We open as a meal assembly kitchen. Customers move from station to station putting together family size dinners, often with a glass of wine in hand.
  • 2009In the middle of the Great Recession we pivot. We start making the meal kits ourselves and delivering them to our customers at home.
  • 2024After 20 years, more than 100,000 customers and over a million dinners, we close the kitchen.

Good Questions

Before you buy

Are these the actual Dinner A'Fare recipes?

Yes. These are the most popular dinners from our 20 years in business, written for a home kitchen.

How long do the meal kits last in the freezer?

Most dinners can be frozen for up to a year. Each chapter tells you how long that protein keeps.

How do I thaw a meal kit?

Move it to the refrigerator a day or two ahead, or set the frozen bags in a bowl of warm water for 15 to 20 minutes right before you cook.

Won't we get tired of eating the same dinner?

You make five kits of a recipe, but you spread them out over months. With four new recipes going into the freezer every month, there is always plenty of variety to pull from.

Can the recipes be made gluten free or dairy free?

Most can, with simple swaps. The book explains what to substitute.
